HR Policy Note 002: What is the process for requesting remote work?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. Employees must submit a remote work request through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance and get sign-off from their direct manager. This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice.
