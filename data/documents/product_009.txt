PRODUCT Policy Note 009: How are feature flags rolled out?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal. Exceptions may be granted on a case-by-case basis with documented approval from the relevant department head. Feature flags are rolled out progressively: 1% -> 10% -> 50% -> 100% of traffic, with automatic rollback if error rates exceed 2%.
