HR Policy Note 014: How is the 401(k) match structured?

This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice. Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. The company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of base salary, vesting immediately with no waiting period.
