IT Policy Note 013: What is the incident severity classification for a production outage?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. A full production outage affecting customer-facing services is classified as Severity 1 (SEV1) and must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
