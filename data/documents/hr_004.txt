HR Policy Note 004: How is the 401(k) match structured?

Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice. The company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of base salary, vesting immediately with no waiting period.
