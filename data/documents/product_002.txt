PRODUCT Policy Note 002: What is the data retention policy for user activity logs?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. User activity logs are retained for 13 months to support trend analysis, then anonymized and aggregated for long-term storage. Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal.
