PRODUCT Policy Note 004: How are feature flags rolled out?

Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Exceptions may be granted on a case-by-case basis with documented approval from the relevant department head. Feature flags are rolled out progressively: 1% -> 10% -> 50% -> 100% of traffic, with automatic rollback if error rates exceed 2%. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort.
