FINANCE Policy Note 006: What is the approval threshold requiring VP sign-off?

Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Any purchase order exceeding $25,000 requires VP-level approval before it can be processed by accounts payable. Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort.
