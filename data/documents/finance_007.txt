FINANCE Policy Note 007: What is the company's fiscal year?

Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal. The company's fiscal year runs from February 1 through January 31, with Q4 close occurring in the last two weeks of January. This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice. Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations.
