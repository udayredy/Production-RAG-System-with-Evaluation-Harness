FINANCE Policy Note 008: What per-diem rate applies to domestic travel?

This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. Domestic business travel is reimbursed at a per-diem rate of $75 per day for meals and incidentals, per the 2026 travel policy. Exceptions may be granted on a case-by-case basis with documented approval from the relevant department head.
