PRODUCT Policy Note 010: What is the median end-to-end latency SLA for the search API?

This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. The search API has a service-level objective of 200ms median end-to-end latency and 800ms at the 99th percentile. Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort.
