HR Policy Note 008: What is the tuition reimbursement cap per year?

The company reimburses up to $5,250 per calendar year for job-related coursework, capped at a 3.0 GPA requirement. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort.
