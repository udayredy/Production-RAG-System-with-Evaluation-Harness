PRODUCT Policy Note 006: How often is the recommendation model retrained?

Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal. The recommendation model is retrained weekly on the previous 90 days of interaction data and validated against a held-out A/B test. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change.
