HR Policy Note 003: What is the tuition reimbursement cap per year?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. The company reimburses up to $5,250 per calendar year for job-related coursework, capped at a 3.0 GPA requirement. Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change.
