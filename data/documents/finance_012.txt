FINANCE Policy Note 012: What is the company's fiscal year?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. The company's fiscal year runs from February 1 through January 31, with Q4 close occurring in the last two weeks of January. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations.
