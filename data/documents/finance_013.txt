FINANCE Policy Note 013: What per-diem rate applies to domestic travel?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice. Domestic business travel is reimbursed at a per-diem rate of $75 per day for meals and incidentals, per the 2026 travel policy. Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal.
