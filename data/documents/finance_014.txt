FINANCE Policy Note 014: How are vendor contracts over $100,000 reviewed?

This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. Vendor contracts exceeding $100,000 in annual value require legal and procurement review, with a target turnaround of 10 business days. This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice.
