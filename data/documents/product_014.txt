PRODUCT Policy Note 014: How are feature flags rolled out?

Employees with questions should contact their business partner or open a ticket through the internal support portal. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. This document supersedes all prior versions and is the single source of truth for current practice. Feature flags are rolled out progressively: 1% -> 10% -> 50% -> 100% of traffic, with automatic rollback if error rates exceed 2%.
