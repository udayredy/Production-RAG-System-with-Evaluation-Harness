FINANCE Policy Note 011: What is the approval threshold requiring VP sign-off?

Any purchase order exceeding $25,000 requires VP-level approval before it can be processed by accounts payable. Historical context: this guidance was last revised after the most recent internal audit and incorporates its recommendations. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change.
