HR Policy Note 013: What is the tuition reimbursement cap per year?

The company reimburses up to $5,250 per calendar year for job-related coursework, capped at a 3.0 GPA requirement. Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. Exceptions may be granted on a case-by-case basis with documented approval from the relevant department head. This policy is reviewed annually by the relevant stakeholders and updated as regulations or business needs change.
