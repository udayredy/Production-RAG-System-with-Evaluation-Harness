FINANCE Policy Note 001: What is the approval threshold requiring VP sign-off?

Related teams should be looped in early when this process intersects with their own workflows to avoid duplicated effort. Any purchase order exceeding $25,000 requires VP-level approval before it can be processed by accounts payable.
